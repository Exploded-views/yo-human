I would run no more commands. Exit 69 with `RESULT failed` means every channel rejected the message for good, and the skill says to stop in that case.

**What I would not do:**
- Retry the `notify`.
- Switch to a different level, such as `nudge` or `murmur`.
- Run `flush`. Nothing is queued, and retrying can't fix rejected credentials.
- Run `yo-human config trust` or `yo-human secret set`, or edit the config or secrets. Those belong to the human.
- Try another tool to get around the failure.

**What I would do:** tell the human directly in my session reply or handoff. The message would say plainly that they have not been reached:

> **I could not reach you through yo-human.** I sent a `siren`-level notification, and both channels rejected it for good (exit 69, `RESULT failed FAKE-ID-3`):
> - phone: dead (the provider rejected the credentials)
> - pager: dead (the provider rejected the request)
>
> **Title:** Checkout failing
> **Ask:** Look at checkout now.
>
> Production checkout is failing and nothing has paged you. The phone credentials look invalid, so please check them and the pager configuration. Only you can fix those. I have not retried or changed the level.

After that I would carry on with whatever I can do safely in the repo. I would not run `yo-human resolve`, because nothing has been fixed and no incident was delivered.
